# CoFID whole-milk volume estimate v1

Status: narrowly admitted source rule for the selected CoFID 2021 record, 28 September 2026. It addresses the `200ml whole milk` basis gap reported in [#144](https://github.com/syamaner/WeeklyHealthReport/issues/144). It does not supply new nutrition data or an exact-10% yoghurt record.

## Decision and source evidence

CoFID 2021 row 1681, food code `12-596`, “Milk, whole, pasteurised, average”, provides nutrition per 100 g and has a blank specific-gravity cell in the original workbook. The app must not portray 1.03 as a CoFID field. [Public Health England's March 2018 Annex A](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5ab8bd9f40f0b67d67479854/Annex__A_the_2018_review_of_the_UK_nutrient_profiling_model.pdf), printed page 95, Table 4, gives whole-milk specific gravity 1.03 and cites the seventh summary edition of McCance and Widdowson. [Dairy UK's whole-milk tables](https://milk.co.uk/nutritional-composition-of-dairy/) also use 1.03 and explicitly explain 100 mL as 103 g; they cite the same book, so this is corroborating use rather than an independent measurement. This supports a practical *estimate* for ordinary whole milk, not a measured density for every brand or condition.

The PHE report states Open Government Licence v3.0 reuse excluding identified third-party material. The [seventh-edition publisher permission notice](https://www.rsc.org/images/Permission%20information_tcm18-241797.pdf) grants reuse of that publication without logos under OGL v2.0 and specifies its attribution. The single factor is admitted with attribution to both publications; no source PDF, table or product image is redistributed. This is a bounded reuse judgement about one factual factor, not a claim that all Dairy UK tables or manufacturer data are licensed. The RSC milk chapter was not independently accessible, so PHE Table 4 is the inspected factor artefact. The source PDFs and the exact CoFID workbook are identified in [the research record](dairy-source-basis-research-v1.md).

The rule manifest `cofid-whole-milk-volume-v1.json` has SHA-256 `bc1b0965edb48e5d09f98092ae6efd85e66fd8ee672880f8de87b650e5514962`; the pinned PHE artefact SHA-256 is `0abf60c447ac94a075e4d2a8065061e64e8d2fd8278959b3fc284ee858510e1b`. The exact nutrition release is `cofid:2021:sha256:436e9445ef2adb2a75f3d7edd51302de3adad25385f9795fc94ba58bd030e97d`, and the only admitted nutrition record is that release's `row:1681:c5ec3e17866aa937`. UHT, seasonal, organic, branded, skimmed, concentrated, powdered and plant milks are outside this rule. A different CoFID release or row cannot inherit it automatically.

## Behaviour and persisted contract

The conversion is offered only after the user selects the exact supported candidate and enters mL. It requires a separate tap, “Use sourced whole-milk volume estimate.” If declined, totals remain unavailable for mL and measured edible grams remain available. The published factor is dimensionless specific gravity; following Dairy UK's documented volume/weight usage, the practical rule estimates 1 mL whole milk as 1.03 g. `200 mL × 1.03 = 206 g estimated`; the existing CoFID per-100-g values are scaled from 206 g without mixing PHE or Dairy UK nutrients into CoFID. For example, 63 kcal/100 g becomes 129.78 kcal before display rounding. Unknown nutrient fields remain unknown.

The existing `QuantityConversionVersion` persists entered volume, converted grams, method version and an independent PHE source-release ID. Save/reopen and archive projection retain that release; a previously saved unsupported mL entry can acquire the rule on reopen without replacing its food identity. A changed amount, candidate, correction or direct-weight route clears the pending estimate, and the user can remove it explicitly. The presentation shows estimated mass and keeps source attribution and licence in expandable details. The source is not a universal density service and does not change the closed nutrition or ledger schema.

## Architecture gate and validation

Pure `FoodQuantityCalculator` checks the unit and finite positive factor. The application exposes a consumer-owned `FoodVolumeConversionOffering` capability; the hash-verified CoFID adapter owns the exact source/record allow-list and manifest. The composition root injects the adapter; presentation requires the user's tap. Store adapters read the conversion source independently of the nutrient source. Domain identity, nutrient provenance, unknown states and immutable versioning remain closed. A future ordinary source adapter can implement the same capability without changing the quantity calculation.

Contract cases cover exact-record allowance, UHT and wrong-unit rejection, no automatic conversion, calculation and edit invalidation, save/reopen and previously saved mL upgrade in memory and GRDB, and archive-state retention. Full simulator, static-analysis and device results must be recorded separately. Device acceptance must check `200ml whole milk`, switching candidates, source disclosure, VoiceOver/larger text, save/reopen and offline reuse. This rule does not resolve the separate exact-10% Greek yoghurt product admission: the named product evidence still needs a reproducible formulation/pack link and source-rights review.
