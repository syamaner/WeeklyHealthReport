# Prospective nutrition NLP acceptance plan v1

Status: prepared collection and review protocol; acceptance not run. This plan
uses the existing metric contract and keeps local deterministic NLP/retrieval
acceptance separate from provider AI, nutrient fidelity, save correctness and
device evaluation. Passing this slice cannot establish acceptance of the whole
nutrition journey or of an LLM component.

## Evidence status and independence

Existing synthetic fixtures, reviewed personal examples, receipts, stock
references and cases used in repairs remain exposed development/regression
material. Human review can improve their references but cannot restore holdout
status. New paraphrases generated by the implementer are synthetic challenges,
not independent natural-query evidence.

Collect a prospective set of 60 distinct scenario groups after freezing the
implementation. A human supplies new natural inputs and intent; a second
reviewer checks references without seeing model/parser outputs or rankings.
If only one reviewer is available, label the result single-reviewer prospective
agreement, with independent adjudication unavailable. Actual intake truth and
measured portion evidence require their own retained evidence.

The proposed stratified roster is a coverage design, not a random population
sample. Do not claim population accuracy or a generalisation confidence interval.
Paraphrases and variants of a scenario remain in one group. Avoid overlap with
exposed product/recipe/intent groups; explicitly distinguish new wording within
known food families from genuinely held-out food/product families. Record source
and scenario overlap rather than claiming that a reused catalogue is unseen.

## Proposed collection roster

| Primary stratum | Distinct groups | Required coverage |
|---|---:|---|
| Plain generic foods | 12 | UK/US sources, fruit/vegetable, protein, dairy and staple families |
| Preparation and form | 10 | Raw/cooked, method distinctions, sliced/whole, unknown source metadata |
| Exact count and fractions | 10 | Whole counts, fractions, missing portion evidence; no inferred grams |
| Explicit mass and volume | 8 | Unit scaling, cross-basis failures, separate density/portion evidence |
| Exact product/source gaps | 8 | Requested exact outcome, unsupported branded identity, labelled alternatives |
| Ambiguous/multiple-food inputs | 6 | Missing meaning, mixtures, household amounts and competing quantities |
| Non-food or malformed context | 6 | Narrative, supplements without form/dose, invalid amounts and controls |

Each group has one primary stratum and additional slice tags. Sixty groups is a
proposed pilot collection size, not proof of statistical sufficiency. If minima
are missing, report incomplete. Admission/privacy invariants also need a
separate synthetic challenge suite; never manufacture adversarial inputs and
then call them natural held-out behaviour.

## Freeze and execution protocol

1. Pin source releases, catalogue hashes, implementation and parser/matcher
   versions, capability profile, case schema and metric/scorer versions.
2. Collect new inputs and intended outcome. Retain original text; separate
   transcription correction, food identity, quantity, preparation and whether
   intake was actually asserted. Preserve unknowns.
3. Review expected routes, quantities, acceptable source record/release pairs,
   useful alternatives and explicit hard negatives before any predictions.
   Exact-product references require exact retained source evidence. Generic
   references require a declared estimate profile. Counts do not supply grams.
4. Freeze reference files and their hashes. Keep uncertainty dispositions in
   the full roster; unavailable references are never quietly excluded from the
   overall decision. Freeze quality thresholds before execution.
5. Run offline against the pinned implementation. Keep predictions hidden from
   reference reviewers until labels are frozen. Emit one output per case/path,
   including failures, blocked routes and unsupported outcomes.
6. Report parsing, source coverage, executed retrieval, all-case journey coverage,
   contradictions and quantity safeguards separately. Preserve a first-run
   snapshot. Any subsequent repair exposes the affected acceptance material;
   retain it as regression evidence and collect a fresh prospective revision.

## Gates and report contract

Admission/privacy violations require zero observed violations in the applicable
suite, as defined by the existing metric contract. Reference/hash integrity,
missing/duplicate outputs or a contaminated holdout invalidate the run. Missing
mandatory suites or reference coverage produce incomplete, not pass.

Quality thresholds remain **unratified**. The frozen synthetic regression gate
is not an independent quality threshold. Before execution, record an approved
threshold/minimum for each required slice and metric. Without those decisions,
report descriptive results with acceptance decision incomplete; do not choose a
threshold after seeing the score.

For this slice report AI01 route confusion, eligible quantity/attribute accuracy
and unsafe prefills; AI02 hit@1/5 over answerable references, source-supported
retrieval and all-case coverage separately. Raw irrelevant/contradictory results
remain quality failures even when admission blocks them. Graded precision or
nDCG requires the contract's reference/ideal coverage; otherwise unavailable.
An empty candidate list is not by itself correct abstention.

Quantity/confirmation probes must preserve original evidence, undecided state,
explicit selection and clearing/stale-selection behaviour. Count-to-mass needs
explicit evidence; independently measured edible weight is a separate user
declaration. Saving and totals need their own synthetic journey suite. No actual
meal-nutrient or usability claim follows from source-backed lookup.

Pin and report every required suite as pass/fail/incomplete/not-run. Zero
observations produce unavailable metrics. Provider execution, judge calibration,
source extraction, OCR, actual saves/totals and physical capture remain separate
profiles, with their own budgets, data authority and references. Do not silently
fill those rows from an offline NLP score.

## Local data boundary

Collection templates and personal review packs live outside Git in owner-only
local storage. No personal input, receipt, stock record or derived judgement is
included in this plan. No provider calls, source acquisition, ledger writes,
credentials, publication, commit or release is authorised by this protocol.

## Local readiness command

[Acceptance readiness CLI](../Tools/NutritionAcceptanceReadiness/README.md) checks
collection coverage, reference declarations, frozen hashes and threshold approvals
without executing or scoring cases. It writes private JSON/Markdown snapshots
outside Git and reports invalid, incomplete or ready for execution. Ready means
the specified preflight checks passed, not semantic reference validity or an
acceptance pass. The prospective scoring adapter remains a separate delivery step.
