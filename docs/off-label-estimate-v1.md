# Open Food Facts plus generic estimate v1 (#464, extends #383)

Status: owner decisions 1 to 6 taken, 11 November 2026. Architecture gate PASS WITH CHANGES (11 November 2026), findings applied. Implemented on branch feat/464-off-label-estimate; invariant review PASS with two fixes applied (11 November 2026).

Extends: [`label-estimate-merge-v1.md`](label-estimate-merge-v1.md), which deferred Open Food Facts in rule 1. Evidence: the owner's device check of TestFlight 0.3.0 (30) on 11 November 2026, and a read-only investigation of the current code on the same day.

## Problem

Godiva "90% Cocoa Dark Chocolate" (Open Food Facts 5000168211954) has no fibre value in Open Food Facts. The app correctly shows fibre as not stated. For a pasted dark chocolate label, Confirm already offers "Estimate unprinted nutrients": the user confirms the cocoa %, picks a bundled reference row (CoFID, USDA or Ciqual), and the missing nutrients are filled as labelled estimates while every value from the label stays as it is. The owner wants the same for Open Food Facts products.

## What the code does today (11 November 2026)

- **What is saved.** An Open Food Facts entry stores the full candidate verbatim: name, brand, values (`augmented` or `bounded`), and per-value provenance naming `open-food-facts` and `off:gtin:<code>`. The source release is a hash of the product JSON or response body, and its ID includes the retrieval time. The response bytes are not kept. The search route's evidence is the typed search text; the barcode route's is the scanned code.
- **What reopen checks.** Nothing source-specific. Pasted labels and label photos are re-verified against their stored text or image manifest, and reviewed web pages have their own check; Open Food Facts entries pass through. Reopen also refuses an estimate layer on any entry that is not a pasted label or photo.
- **Where the estimate is gated to labels.** `LabelEstimateOffer.applies` (route), the composition root (only the pasted-label and photo Confirm models get `saveEstimate`), `FoodConfirmationViewModel.offersLabelEstimate`, the save guard (`invalidLabelEstimate`), and reopen. The compatibility rule and the merge do not check the route.
- **Basis.** Open Food Facts candidates are per 100 g or per 100 ml. The merge needs a mass, so per 100 ml products offer no estimate.
- **Only eight keys are read** from Open Food Facts: energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat, saturates, fibre, sugars and sodium (each where present). Minerals, vitamins and every other key are unknown, so an estimate would mostly add those. Every other key is unknown, and a value in a unit the reader cannot convert is unknown with reason `missingConversion`.
- **Barcode aliases.** Logging an Open Food Facts barcode product saves a personal-library alias; a rescan returns that entry's saved nutrients as a library row.

## Proposed rules

1. **Which entries.** Rule 1 of `label-estimate-merge-v1` admits a third route through one closed application-layer predicate, `OpenFoodFactsEstimateSource.applies`: the candidate's release has `sourceID == "open-food-facts"`; every stated value's provenance names that release and the candidate's record; the evidence is a barcode or a search text. A personal-library rescan row (rule 5) whose stated values all carry Open Food Facts provenance also qualifies. The name contains "chocolate" and none of milk, white or ruby, and the basis is per 100 g. Per 100 ml products, mixed provenance and corrected entries offer no estimate. The predicate is used by `LabelEstimateOffer.applies`, the save guard and reopen, never re-implemented.
2. **Evidence of record.** For an Open Food Facts entry, the stored candidate is the evidence of record: the product can change remotely, so the app never re-fetches it, and the response bytes are not kept, so the candidate itself cannot be checked against its source (owner decision 1).
   - **Reopen:** for an entry with a layer, the resolution nutrients must equal `LabelEstimateLayer.expectedNutrients(saved, label: stored candidate)`, plus the existing estimate-layer checks (release present, provenance names it, compatibility rerun on the stored cocoa %). The reopen refusal of a layer on a non-label entry widens to "label or Open Food Facts". An Open Food Facts entry **without** a layer keeps today's path exactly, so no existing entry can stop opening.
   - **Edit save:** editing the time or amount of a layered Open Food Facts entry rechecks the layer the same way before saving, as the label branch does today. A food details correction drops the layer, as for labels.
3. **What may be filled.** Every key the product leaves unknown, whatever the reason, as the merge does for labels: `notDeclared`, and also `missingConversion`, where Open Food Facts lists a value in a unit the app cannot convert (owner decision 2). Each filled value is marked as an estimate with its reference source, as for labels. Developer tools keeps the product's original unknown reason beside the estimate, so an unconverted Open Food Facts value stays traceable.
4. **Cocoa % pre-fill.** From the product name, with the existing label reader (as for pasted labels), plus the "N% dark chocolate" display reading from [`cocoa-exact-match-v1.md`](cocoa-exact-match-v1.md) rule 1, so "95% Dark Chocolate" pre-fills 95. Otherwise the field is empty and the user types the percentage. The origin is `name` or `typed`, as today.
5. **Barcode rescan.** The personal-library alias keeps the Open Food Facts values only (owner decision 3). Today the rescan row is built from the entry's resolution nutrients, which would be the merged values, so the barcode library record gains the first decision's candidate nutrients (GRDB and in-memory stores alike). When the entry has a layer, the rescan row uses those nutrients and drops the generic release. The rescan row keeps all-Open Food Facts provenance, so rule 1's predicate admits it and Confirm offers the estimate again. Pasted labels and the Open Food Facts search route have no alias.
6. **Copy.** Every "label" phrase in the estimate section and on the saved entry gets a product form, chosen by a route discriminator taken from the first decision's source: "Estimate missing nutrients" (section title), "Unchanged from the product: …", "Not read from Open Food Facts · Estimated" (true for every reason: not listed, a unit the app cannot convert, or a value it could not parse), and on the saved entry "Partly estimated · Open Food Facts values, with missing nutrients estimated from US reference". The re-log notice reads "Logs the Open Food Facts values only. …". Developer tools shows each filled key's original reason, read from the stored first-decision candidate (`originalReasons` on the saved entry's estimate detail); nothing new is stored.
6a. **Wiring.** `saveEstimate` is passed to the Confirm models of the Open Food Facts search route, the barcode lookup and the library rescan, not to re-log or quick add. Gating stays in the predicate.
6b. **Pre-fill (decision 4).** An Open Food Facts-only branch reads `CocoaPercentPolicy.displayBand(name:description: nil, recordID: nil)` on the product name; the shared label pre-fill is not changed. This depends on `cocoa-percent-policy-v3` ([`cocoa-exact-match-v1.md`](cocoa-exact-match-v1.md)) merging first.
7. **Format.** The layer is the same `confirmFoodV4` layer; the registry and archive already work for any route. Contract 9 and migration `v9_off_label_estimate`: see decision 5. Comments saying the layer is "never Open Food Facts" and merge contract rule 1 are updated in the same change. "Partly estimated" marking, Health, Copy all and the daily export follow section 8 of the merge contract as they are. No new egress: the estimate pool stays on the device.

## Tests (to be fixed before code)

- Offer rules: chocolate, milk, white and ruby names; per 100 g and per 100 ml; corrected entries.
- Predicate: admits Open Food Facts search, barcode and library-rescan rows; refuses mixed provenance and corrected rows.
- Merge: `notDeclared`, `missingConversion`, `noCompatibleSource` and legacy-sodium keys are filled and marked as estimates; every Open Food Facts value (including a zero and a bound) is kept; Developer tools shows each original reason.
- Edit save: changing the time or amount of a layered Open Food Facts entry with an altered resolution is refused.
- Save and reopen: an Open Food Facts entry with a layer saves V4 and reopens; a tampered resolution, generic row or cocoa % is refused; an Open Food Facts entry without a layer saves and reopens byte for byte as today.
- Barcode: after logging with an estimate, a rescan's library row holds no estimated value and no generic release, and Confirm offers the estimate; in both the GRDB and in-memory stores.
- Re-log and quick add of a layered Open Food Facts entry show the Open Food Facts notice and no estimate section.
- Pre-fill (name only, the variant's source text ignored): "90% Cocoa Dark Chocolate" pre-fills 90, "95% Dark Chocolate" pre-fills 95, "Dark chocolate with almonds" gives none; pasted-label pre-fill results are unchanged.
- Copy: the product forms on Confirm, the saved entry, Food Log row and re-log notice; VoiceOver labels name each estimate and its source.
- Journey: search "dark chocolate 90%", pick the Godiva product, confirm 90, choose a reference row, see fibre estimated, Log, reopen.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Evidence of record:** accept the stored Open Food Facts candidate as the evidence that reopen checks against, since the product is never re-fetched. **Decided by the owner, 11 November 2026:** yes.
2. **Unconvertible values:** leave `missingConversion` keys unknown rather than estimating them. Recommended: yes. **Decided by the owner, 11 November 2026:** no; estimate them too (rule 3).
3. **Barcode rescan:** keep estimates out of the personal-library alias, so a rescan offers the estimate again. **Decided by the owner, 11 November 2026:** yes.
4. **Pre-fill:** the name reader plus the "N% dark chocolate" phrase, no pre-fill from the search's own percentage. **Decided by the owner, 11 November 2026:** yes, name only.
5. **Version compatibility (open).** An older build that opens a store or archive holding a layered Open Food Facts entry would show an integrity failure for it, because older builds refuse a layer on a non-label entry. Either (a) contract 9, used only when a layer sits on an Open Food Facts entry, with a metadata-only migration so older builds decline the store cleanly, as `v8_label_estimate` did; or (b) keep contract 8 and record that going back to an older build is unsupported. Recommended: (a). **Decided by the owner, 11 November 2026:** (a). `foodContractVersion` 9 and a metadata-only migration `v9_off_label_estimate` (`food-ledger-v9`, user_version 9); `minimumContractVersion` returns 9 only when a layer's first decision is an Open Food Facts source, so stores and archives without one keep exporting 6, 7 or 8; historical migration hashes unchanged. Release notes repeat build 30's warning: back up before installing, because older builds cannot open the food log afterwards.
6. **Food details on Open Food Facts products.** Every Open Food Facts product arrives with its food details (preparation, bone, skin, drained, packing medium, fortification) unknown, so Log needs a food details correction, and rule 1 would then drop the estimate: no estimated Open Food Facts entry could ever be saved (found while implementing, 11 November 2026). **Decided by the owner, 11 November 2026:** on an Open Food Facts entry, a correction that only fills in or changes food details keeps the estimate, when the product name, brand and every nutrient value are unchanged. The estimate depends only on the name, the cocoa % and the values, so food details cannot invalidate it. Any change to the name, brand or a value drops the estimate, as today. Pasted labels and label photos are unchanged.
   - One predicate, `OpenFoodFactsEstimateSource.isIdentityOnlyCorrection`, compares the correction's name, brand, full nutrient set and serving basis with the selected candidate's; Confirm, the save guard and the assertion all read `admitsLayer`, which uses it.
   - Narrowed after the invariant review (11 November 2026, programme lead under decision 6's intent): a correction that marks the product fortified or changes its item class (for example to a supplement) also drops the estimate, because the bundled reference rows are unfortified plain foods. The description may change. The withdrawal note on this route reads "Estimates are not available after the name, brand or values are changed. Missing nutrients stay unknown."
   - Limits (recorded 11 November 2026): reopen re-checks the values, the cocoa assertion and compatibility, but cannot re-check the name and brand rule, because the stored candidate keeps neither (they live on the product version); that rule is enforced at save. Correcting an already saved layered entry still drops the layer, as today; decision 6 applies to the correction made before the first Log.
