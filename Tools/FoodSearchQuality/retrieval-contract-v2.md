# Generic ranking contract v2 — issue #160

Freeze these 72 development scenarios and record judgements before replacing the narrow bonuses. Preserve v1/v2 labels and all runs. Re-score old and new queries against the same v3 judgements. The baseline is actual bundled adapters/composite from build 9 commit b18482b. Results remain same-author exposed development evidence.

Ranking policy applies the same token-based representation preference across foods: primary food before ingredient mentions, fewer unrequested specialisations (flavour, concentrated form, unusual species) before specialised records, and ordinary presentation descriptors such as plain, average, whole, white or regular before unspecified specialised variants. Descriptors express presentation preferences, not confirmed identity or universal food equivalence. Explicit query descriptors take precedence; raw/cooked selection remains a hard constraint. Apply the same comparator before both adapter result limits and across composite results; regional source order is a stable tiebreak, not a relevance score. More detailed numeric variant matching is deferred; retain shipped hints without adding new variant mechanisms.

Labels: generic yoghurt prefers plain (grade 3), flavoured alternatives grade 1, even for numeric-fat queries in this generic-ranking phase. Numeric mismatch exposure is still reported separately; it is not a generic-ranking failure or identity acceptance. Milk prefers ordinary whole records (3), other ordinary cow's milk (2), concentrated/flavoured alternatives (1), human/sheep/goat (0) for unspecified queries. Rice prefers white (3), brown (2), red/other named rice (1) for unspecified queries, with raw and cooked both relevant until the user confirms basis. These are explicit presentation preferences from device feedback. No mass, density, portion or nutrient assumptions follow. Explicit-qualified scenarios require the named descriptor. Transfer families give primary named food grade 3 and ingredient-only mentions grade 1. Keep complete source-record labels and source-specific coverage denominators.

Hit@1/5 and MRR use grade >=2, nDCG@5 uses gain 2^grade-1 with catalogue-wide ideal, all metrics retain coverage gaps separately. Per-family/source confusion matrices and explicit qualifiers test regressions; no independent acceptance claim.

Quantity handoff is a separate native-flow contract: exact g, kg converted to g, mL and count remain editable after selecting a non-first candidate. Capture a parsed-quantity snapshot with the selected confirmation; own a stable confirmation model throughout navigation. Count and cross-basis units require explicit supported conversion before saving. Clarification/rejection never prefills exact amounts or saves. Do not assert a device cause until reproduced; retain a device retest checklist.
