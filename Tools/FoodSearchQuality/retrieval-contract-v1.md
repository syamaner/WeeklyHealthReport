# Ranked retrieval development contract v1

Judgements are defined from the bundled catalogues before the first ranked run or tuning. Same-author exposed synthetic development scenarios; no independent acceptance, production accuracy or nutritional equivalence claim. Historical fixtures stay unchanged.

Grade 3: requested food family; for unqualified Greek yoghurt prefer plain records over flavoured alternatives. Numeric fat requests require exact source grams of fat per 100 g for this grade, without claiming product identity. Ribeye includes the named cut only, and explicit preparation constraints must agree. Clementine includes only named clementines. Grade 1: same Greek yoghurt family but flavoured (unqualified query), or numerically different fat (numeric query). These are tentative alternatives requiring explicit review. Grade 0: unrelated or hard preparation conflict. Missing labels are grade 0. No recipe, portion mass, density or nutrient inference.

Labels contain full source record IDs, including all relevant records in each catalogue rather than just returned records. Hit@1/5 and MRR use grade >=2; nDCG@5 uses gain 2^grade-1 against the complete labelled eligible source catalogue. Metrics exclude exact-coverage gaps from the ranking denominator, reporting them separately. Grade-1-only requests may return explicitly marked alternatives and are not expected to return empty. No-result accuracy uses cases with no positive grades in the source catalogue. Coverage is source-specific and composite coverage is their union. It does not measure worldwide food availability.

Incorrect variant exposure is counted at ranks 1 and 5 for numeric fat requests and remains a separate metric even when alternatives are labelled. The binary confusion matrix compares catalogue relevance availability (grade >=2) to actual relevant retrieval (grade >=2 anywhere returned); per-family and per-source denominators remain explicit. Preserve original query, full source releases, whole candidates including nutrient provenance, and variant notes in the replay. Identical scenarios and labels score before and after. No metric thresholds establish independent acceptance.

Architecture: Swift XCTest replay invokes both real bundled adapters and their composite; Python scorer reads frozen record judgements. Existing pure candidate assessment supplies variant hints; infrastructure adapters own bounded retrieval. No provider, credential, upload or dataset changes.
